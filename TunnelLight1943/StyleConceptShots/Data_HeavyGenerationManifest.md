## Shared art direction

Preserve the original gameplay scene, character relationships, 1943 rural north-China setting, side-view spatial logic, minimal HUD and layered handmade-paper visual language. Add emotional weight through composition and material detail rather than a uniform dark overlay:

- make people smaller and more vulnerable than walls, earth, fortifications and empty ground;
- use low ash-gray skies, thick soil bands, narrow passages and compressed spacing;
- show exhausted posture, damaged plaster, soot, warped timber, dead branches and sparse debris;
- restrict warmth to tiny weak amber lamps and very restrained rust-red clothing accents;
- let empty space imply absence, exposure and scarcity rather than calm;
- retain flat geometric silhouettes, fibrous paper texture and immediate gameplay readability.

Exclude gore, horror imagery, theatrical crying, heroic action poses, glossy cinematic lighting, photorealism, modern objects, added text, logos and watermarks.
