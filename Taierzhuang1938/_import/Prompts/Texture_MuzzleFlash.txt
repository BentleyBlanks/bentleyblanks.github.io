## Front (Texture_MuzzleFlashFrontSource) — atlas cells 0-3
Photorealistic high-speed photography reference sheet for a game visual-effects texture.
A 2x2 grid of FOUR DIFFERENT muzzle blasts of a 1930s military bolt-action rifle (7.92 mm), each seen from DIRECTLY IN FRONT of the muzzle, looking straight down the bore axis, so the gas cloud spreads radially around a centre point.
Each blast: hot propellant gas bursting out as a soft, turbulent, asymmetric cloud; a small bright pale-yellow core at the centre, warm yellow-orange body, dull orange-red ragged wispy edges that fade into thin translucent grey smoke; 3 to 5 soft irregular lobes, never a clean star; a few tiny glowing unburnt powder sparks flying outward. Real photographic detail, like a frame from a 1000 fps camera.
Strictly NOT cartoon, no sharp geometric spikes, no lens flare, no glow rings, no gun, no barrel, no hands, no text, no grid lines, no borders.
Background is pure black (#000000) everywhere outside the flames. Each blast is centred in its own quarter with a generous black margin so no flame touches the edges of its quarter. Square image, 1024x1024.

Photorealistic high-speed photography reference sheet for a game visual-effects texture.

Real photographic detail like a frame from a 1000 fps camera. Strictly NOT cartoon, no geometric spikes, no lens flare, no gun, no barrel, no hands, no text, no grid lines, no borders.
Pure black background (#000000) everywhere outside the flames, generous black margin so no flame touches the edges of its quarter. Square image, 1024x1024.
